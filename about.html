About Gloomy Bridal

Gloomy Bridal is a wedding photography and cinematic film studio based in Bogura. We create honest, elegant and cinematic visual stories for couples across Bangladesh.

Our work is led by emotion: the glance, the hands, the laughter, the family, the quiet pause before everything changes.
